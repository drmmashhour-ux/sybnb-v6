Delete your Rezekme account

Applies to the Rezekme Stays and Rezekme Rides apps and websites (stay.rezekme.com, rides.rezekme.com). One Rezekme account works in both.

Delete it yourself (immediate)

Rezekme Stays (app or stay.rezekme.com): sign in, open the account menu (top of the screen) and choose Delete account, then confirm.

Rezekme Rides (app or rides.rezekme.com): sign in, open Profile and choose Delete account, then confirm.

Or ask us

Email support@rezekme.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Rezekme account". We confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, name, phone number link, password, profile photo and ID document are removed, and you are signed out on every device. You cannot sign in again with that account.
  • Your saved card reference, saved addresses, favourites, notification devices and marketing consent are removed.
  • Your listings are taken off the site. A listing with an upcoming confirmed stay stays until our support team has settled that stay with the guest.
  • In-ride safety recordings are deleted automatically after 7 days.

What we keep, and why

  • Records of bookings, rides, payments, refunds and invoices, without your name or email, for as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention law requires.
  • Reviews you wrote may stay visible without your name.
  • Drivers: the documents submitted to drive (licence, vehicle papers) are kept for as long as transport and safety rules require. Ask support@rezekme.com to delete them after that.

حذف حسابك في Rezekme

Rezekme Stays: سجّل الدخول، افتح قائمة الحساب أعلى الشاشة واختر حذف الحساب ثم أكّد.

Rezekme Rides: سجّل الدخول، افتح حسابي واختر حذف الحساب ثم أكّد.

أو راسلنا على support@rezekme.com من بريد حسابك، وسنحذفه خلال 30 يوماً.

نحذف بريدك واسمك وكلمة المرور وصورتك ووثيقة هويتك وبطاقتك المحفوظة وعناوينك ومفضلتك، ونُنهي كل الجلسات، ونُزيل إعلاناتك من الموقع (الإعلان الذي عليه إقامة مؤكدة قادمة يبقى حتى يسوّيها فريق الدعم مع الضيف). نحتفظ بسجلات الحجوزات والرحلات والمدفوعات بدون اسمك أو بريدك طالما يفرض القانون ذلك.
