## 1. What SYBNB is
SYBNB is an online **intermediary platform** that connects users with third parties across several
divisions: short-term **Daily Stays**, **Monthly Rentals**, **Buy Property**, **Cars**,
**Marketplace** goods/services, **New Construction** projects, **SR Ride** transportation, and
**Advertising**. SYBNB **facilitates** discovery, communication, listing moderation, and
payment/verification workflows. **SYBNB does not own, operate, sell, let, drive, or control** any
property, vehicle, product, service, or ride offered by users, and is **not a party** to the
resulting agreement between a buyer/guest/renter/rider and a seller/host/landlord/dealer/developer/
driver. `[[LEGAL/OWNER TO CONFIRM the intermediary characterization and any licensing/regulatory

## 7. Wallet & promotional gifts
The in-app **Wallet** and **Gifts** are a **platform-funded promotional credit** mechanism. A gift
credits the recipient's wallet as a promotion; it is **not** a transfer of the sender's money,
**not** stored-value/e-money, and **not** redeemable for cash. Gifts are phone-targeted, code-
protected, single-use, and **expire**. `[[LEGAL/OWNER TO CONFIRM: promotional-credit terms, expiry,
