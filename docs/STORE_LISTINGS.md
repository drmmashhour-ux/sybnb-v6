## PRIVACY / DATA SAFETY (both apps)

These answers mirror the published privacy policy. Confirm against your final policy before submitting.

**Data collected & linked to the user**
- Contact info: name, email, phone number.
- Identity (drivers, and where required): government-ID image, profile photo; drivers also vehicle details.
- Financial info: payment method and transaction reference/proof; wallet balance. **No full card numbers are stored** — card payments are handled by the payment provider (Stripe).
- Location: precise location — rider pickup/drop-off and position during an active trip; driver continuous precise GPS while online. Used for ride matching, dispatch, navigation and safety.
- User content: in-app messages, support requests, safety/SOS reports, reviews.
- Identifiers & diagnostics: device model/OS, app version, IP, push token, basic diagnostics.

**Use**: app functionality (matching, dispatch, fares), payments & payouts, identity/vehicle verification, safety & fraud prevention, dispute resolution, service communications/push (with consent), legal compliance.

**Sharing**: between rider and driver for a trip only; with operators/admins for review and safety; with service providers (hosting, maps, email, payment reconciliation) under confidentiality; with authorities where required by law. **No data sold; no third-party advertising.**

**Security**: encryption in transit, hashed credentials, access controls, restricted operator access.

**Deletion**: in-app self-service account deletion (Account → Delete account) and by emailing info@sybnb.app. → Google Play "data deletion" URL requirement is satisfied by the in-app deletion plus the email route; provide a web deletion-request page at your public domain if Play asks for a URL.

**Apple App Privacy "nutrition label" summary**
- Data used to track you: **None.**
- Data linked to you: Contact Info, Financial Info (payment method/reference, not card number), Location (precise), User Content, Identifiers, Usage/Diagnostics, Sensitive Info (ID document — map to "Other").
- Data not linked to you: Diagnostics (if applicable).
